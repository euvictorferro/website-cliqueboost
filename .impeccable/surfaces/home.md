## Direction contract

THESIS: A marketing agency is usually pitched as a mood board with a soft gradient hero; Clique Boost is pitched as an installed electrical panel — six labeled circuit breakers (Websites, Tráfego, Social Media, Automações, Design Gráfico, Brand Guide) wired into one main breaker marked BOOSTCONNECT. Refuses the category default (dark gradient SaaS hero, vague promise) and its predictable opposite (warm cream editorial "boutique agency" page).

OWN-WORLD: Graphite (#1c1e22-ish) and off-white (#f2efe8-ish) panel plates as page grounds; engraved/embossed label-tape typography (P-touch/Dymo style, tracked-out caps) for eyebrows, tags, and section labels; amber, red, and green indicator LEDs as the only saturated accents, used exactly like real panel status lights (on/standby/fault); brass/copper hardware details (screws, hinges, terminal screws) as recurring texture motif; condensed industrial/technical grotesk for display headlines (not Inter/DM Sans/Space Grotesk defaults — an industrial stencil-adjacent condensed face) paired with a monospace for data readouts and labels; thin schematic wiring lines (1px, right-angle routing) connecting related elements instead of decorative dividers; layout gridded to a panel-module unit (repeating rhythm like breaker slots on a rail).

STORY: Visitor lands and reads the business as engineered infrastructure, not a vibe pitch — six real pain points, each its own circuit, wired into one main breaker (the BoostConnect method). Believes: this is a serious, integrated operation, not six freelancers stitched together. Does: flips into a service's breaker to read its problem/solution/process, or throws the main switch (primary CTA) to talk to a consultant.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed panel-plate composition. Six services render as individual breaker switches in a row/grid, each labeled with an engraved plate, a small numbered slot, and a status LED (all lit amber = "available", per RAISE 1 below — off states are never simply hidden). Thin schematic lines converge all six toward one larger central breaker plate labeled "BOOSTCONNECT". Headline is engraved directly onto the plate metal. Primary CTA is styled as the main breaker's physical toggle (flip affordance, on/off states). Secondary CTA ("Falar com um consultor") is a labeled terminal/screw-post button beside it.

RAISES (borrowed discipline from declined/competitive challengers, named):
- RAISE FROM SEVEN-SEGMENT/CATHODE FAMILIES (declined — too close to the generic neon-on-black AI cliché the calibration warns against): inactive/off states get as much deliberate design care as active ones. An unselected or unavailable circuit renders dim-but-legible, never disappears or goes fully blank.
- RAISE FROM BRICK-BUILD-INSTRUCTIONS (competitive — wins on process clarity, loses overall on audience trust/premium register for a B2B closing context): each service page's "Como funciona" section adopts a numbered exploded-step diagram grammar — big numeral tags plus thin wordless connector/guide lines — replacing the current plain 4-card grid.

FORM: candidate 5 of the grounded direction list (electrical breaker/control-panel world: signals-instruments family, adjacent to industrial/utility panel systems), seed key 858d637f, mode persuade.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
